# Decision Policy Card

`Expected Revenue(T) = P(Conversion | X,T) × E[Spend | Conversion=1,X,T]`

`Utility(T) = Expected Revenue(T) − Treatment Cost(T)`

Production selects the action with maximum frozen utility among No Email, Women's Email and Men's Email. Uncertainty is represented by bootstrap policy intervals and frozen ensemble action stability. Bandit results are simulation only.
